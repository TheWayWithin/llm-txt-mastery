## Lessons Learned

### 1. Feature Without Marketing = Invisible Feature

**What happened**: We built sophisticated framework detection that captures 90%+ content from modern JavaScript sites - a dramatic competitive advantage. But it wasn't mentioned anywhere on the site.

**The insight**: A feature customers don't know about might as well not exist. Doug Hall's principle: if you can't communicate the benefit clearly, the feature has no value.

**The fix**: Added framework detection messaging to 5 key sections using Overt Benefit (works on any technology) + Dramatic Difference (competitors miss 60% of your content).

### 2. Doug Hall Works for Technical Products

**The formula**:
- **Overt Benefit**: Clear, immediate value ("Works on ANY website")
- **Dramatic Difference**: Quantified differentiation ("90%+ vs 40%")
- **Reason to Believe**: Technical proof ("React, Next.js, Vue, Angular, Astro...")

**Result**: Framework support is now a visible differentiator in pricing decisions, not hidden technical capability.

### 3. Pricing Cards Are Decision Points

**Observation**: The pricing cards are where users decide to upgrade. Adding "All 15+ frameworks supported" to paid tiers creates clear upgrade justification vs free tier's "Works on any website".
