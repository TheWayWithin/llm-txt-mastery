## The llms.txt Validator Problem Nobody Was Talking About

I've been building LLM.txt Mastery, a platform to help websites optimize for AI visibility.

One thing kept bugging me: **most llms.txt validators give incorrect results for modern websites.**

Here's why:

40-60% of the web now uses JavaScript frameworks like React, Next.js, Vue, or Angular. These sites render content dynamically - the HTML your browser requests is just an empty shell.

Traditional validators fetch that shell and call it a day. They miss everything.

This means:
- Developers get false confidence their files are "valid"
- Content that AI actually sees vs. what's documented doesn't match
- The whole point of llms.txt breaks down
