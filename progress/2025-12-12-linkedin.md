I rewrote my signup page today. No idea if it'll work.

Found a framework called "Marketing Physics" by Doug Hall. The idea: features don't convert, outcomes do.

So I changed this:
"Solo Plan - 200 pages per analysis"

To this:
"Stop losing customers to the competitors AI recommends instead of you"

Feels more compelling. But I'm not my customer, so who knows.

Three bets I'm making:

1. Defaulted to Growth tier instead of Free
Theory: people anchor on the default. Counter-argument: maybe it just annoys people?

2. Moved the signup form below trust signals
Theory: AIDA says desire before action. Counter-argument: maybe people just want to sign up fast?

3. Made "Most Popular" actually visible
This one I'm more confident about. If you're recommending something, actually recommend it.

I don't have enough traffic to A/B test properly. So I'm shipping it and watching what happens.

If it works, I'll share the data. If it flops, I'll share that too.

That's the deal with building in public. You don't just share the wins.

Anyone else running conversion experiments right now? What are you testing?
