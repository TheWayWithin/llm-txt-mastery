## What AI Crawlers Actually Need

Here's something most people don't realize: having an llms.txt file at `/llms.txt` on your website is only step one. AI crawlers also need to be **told** the file exists through discovery mechanisms:

1. **A `robots.txt` directive** — a line that says `Llms-Txt: https://yourdomain.com/llms.txt`
2. **An HTML discovery tag** — a `<link>` tag in your page's `<head>` section
3. **The right content type** — the file served as `text/plain`

Without these, crawlers have to guess that `/llms.txt` exists. Most won't bother.

This is almost certainly why the benchmark scores us 3.1. We generate a good file. But we don't help you deploy it in a way that AI systems can actually find.

## Sprint 12: The Complete Loop

So we're building it. All of it. Sprint 12 is the biggest feature sprint we've ever planned — five phases that transform LLM.txt Mastery from a generator into a complete deployment platform.

### 1. Eating Our Own Dog Food

First, the embarrassing stuff. Our own site — llmtxtmastery.com — is missing the discovery mechanisms we should be showcasing. No `Llms-Txt:` directive in our robots.txt. No `<link>` tag in our HTML. Our own llms.txt file was auto-generated with generic descriptions.

We're fixing that first. If we're going to tell you how to deploy llms.txt properly, our site needs to be the reference implementation.

### 2. Real Spec Compliance Validation (Not Just "Is It Valid Markdown?")

Our validator currently checks structure, URL accessibility, robots.txt conflicts, and gives a quality score. That's useful but it's not enough.

We're adding full specification compliance checking:

- **Does it have the required sections?** Title, description, URLs — the spec requires these.
- **Is the content actually useful to an LLM?** We'll score descriptiveness, completeness, and accuracy against the live site.
- **Is it fresh?** We'll compare the llms.txt content against your current pages and flag stale entries — pages that have been removed, titles that have changed, content that's drifted.
- **Is it the right size?** We'll calculate token counts and tell you whether your file fits common LLM context windows.
- **What format is it?** We'll detect whether you're using standard, full, or mini conventions.

And instead of just a numeric score, you'll get a **compliance grade**: A, B, C, or D. With specific, actionable fixes for each issue.

### 3. Three Formats From One Crawl

Right now we generate one format. But the llms.txt ecosystem has evolved:

- **llms.txt** (standard) — structured summary with page descriptions
- **llms-full.txt** — complete markdown extraction of all content, for full LLM ingestion
- **llms-mini.txt** — token-constrained version with just the essentials, for small context windows

After Sprint 12, one analysis generates all three. Preview them side by side. See the token count for each. Download individually or as a zip.

No re-crawling. No re-analyzing. One crawl, three outputs.

### 4. The Deployment Panel (This Is the Big One)

This is what should move the benchmark score the most.

After you generate your files, instead of just a download button, you'll see a **"Deploy Your llms.txt"** panel with three steps:

**Step 1: Download your files.** Choose your format(s).

**Step 2: Add the HTML discovery tag.** We generate the exact code for YOUR domain — not `example.com`, your actual URL. One-click copy.

**Step 3: Update your robots.txt.** Same thing — the exact directive with your domain pre-filled.

And then: a **"Verify My Deployment"** button that checks all five discovery mechanisms automatically:

- Is the file accessible?
- Is the HTML tag present?
- Is the robots.txt directive there?
- Is the content type correct?
- Does the deployed file match what we generated?

5 out of 5? You're fully deployed. 3 out of 5? We tell you exactly which steps you missed.

We're even building platform-specific guides for WordPress, Shopify, Squarespace, Wix, Webflow, Next.js, and static sites. Not "upload to your root directory" — actual step-by-step instructions for each platform.
