**Current Status**:

- Product operational and stable

- **Why**: Current traffic volume + solo founder constraints require more realistic timeline

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: Landing Page Optimization Shows No Lift

**Mitigation**:

- Measure baseline before Week 1
- Phased approach allows stopping after Week 1 if no improvement
- User testing can identify issues
  **Backup**: Professional UX audit ($1,000-2,000)

### Risk 2: Product Hunt Doesn't Deliver Traffic

**Mitigation**:

- Prepare assets professionally
- Engage beta users for support
- Offer compelling launch-day deal
  **Backup**: Focus on content marketing and WordPress plugin instead

### Risk 3: Conversion Remains Low Despite Optimization

**Mitigation**:

- A/B testing framework to iterate
- User interviews to understand objections
- Pricing experiments
  **Backup**: Consider pricing adjustment or different target audience

### Risk 4: Solo Founder Burnout

**Mitigation**:

- Strict 40 hour/week limit
- Phased approach with decision gates
- Automate support and onboarding
  **Backup**: Extend timeline, reduce scope, or hire VA for support

### Risk 5: Competition Intensifies

**Mitigation**:

- Focus on FreecalcHub success story (unique proof)
- WordPress plugin creates distribution advantage
- Speed to market (7 months to $5K is fast)
  **Backup**: Pivot to white-label B2B model for agencies

### Gate 1: After Week 1 (Oct 11)

- **NO**: Pause, conduct user testing, reassess before Week 2
