## Implementation Notes

- **Task 1 (titles)** is purely in the output formatting layer (`routes.ts`). No scoring changes needed. Should be straightforward.
- **Task 2 (scoring)** requires threading the CSR detection flag through the analysis pipeline. The detection already exists in `sitemap.ts` -- it just needs to reach `openai.ts` scoring functions.
- **Task 3 (grammar)** is a one-liner in two places: `routes.ts` lines 1939 and 2575. Conditional plural.
- Tasks 1 and 3 must NOT affect SSR sites -- boosting and title changes only trigger when CSR is detected or titles are actually identical. Task 3 applies universally.
