# Outreach A/B Testing

**Current Test:** First Line
**Start Date:** 2026-02-03

---

## First Line Variants

### Variant A: Vulnerability-First (The Shy Dev)
> I'm a developer who built something for AI optimization — but I'm painfully shy and honestly terrible at outreach. You clearly know what you're doing here.

**Hypothesis:** Disarms sales radar, invites empathy, makes them want to help.

### Variant B: Curiosity-First (The Question)
> Saw your work on [Site] — quick question: have you thought about how AI assistants see your sites?

**Hypothesis:** Leads with their work (flattering), opens curiosity loop, more conventional but proven.

## Full Templates

```
Any tips on how I could do this outreach better would genuinely be more valuable than a conversion 😅

— Jamie
```

### Template B (Curiosity + Advice Ask)
```
Hi [Name],

Saw your work on [Site] — quick question: have you thought about how AI assistants see your sites?

I built llmtxtmastery.com for exactly this — but I'm new to outreach and honestly learning as I go. You clearly know what you're doing.

Would love your honest take on whether this would resonate with someone like you. Tips on my approach welcome too 😅

— Jamie
```
